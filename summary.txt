# School Accessories Mart

## Project Feature Summary

## Project Overview

School Accessories Mart is an e-commerce platform for selling school uniforms and accessories.

The platform has two user roles:

* Customer
* Admin

Customers can browse uniforms based on their school, add products to a cart, place orders, and track deliveries.

Admins manage schools, products, categories, orders, and deliveries.

---
